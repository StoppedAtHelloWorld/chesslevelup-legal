Privacy policy

How the mobile application stores progress, handles reminders and recognises purchases.

EnglishFrançaisEspañolDeutschItaliano

English

Data stored on your device

Your progress, answers, preferences and a technical history limited to 100 events remain on your device. The app does not send them to a ChessLevelUp server.

Training reminders

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can disable them at any time in the app or device settings.

Purchases

When purchases are available, Google Play or Apple processes the payment. The app does not receive your card details and stores only the purchase status needed to recognise access.

Tracking and advertising

The app contains no advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics service.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until the app is uninstalled. Because the app has no user account, ChessLevelUp keeps no server copy that could be restored or deleted.

Public policy hosting

This page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection logs under its own privacy statement.
